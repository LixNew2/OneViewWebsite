Questions, answered.

Everything people ask before installing OneView.

What is OneView?

OneView is a mobile app for creators that brings the stats of your YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Twitch accounts into one place: views, followers, revenue and lives, one chart for every platform, goals, the best time to post, your comments, and one button to publish a video (or your Twitch clips) to all your accounts at once.

Which platforms and phones does OneView support?

OneView works with YouTube, TikTok, Instagram (professional accounts) and Twitch. The app runs on iPhone and Android, with Home Screen widgets on both.

Is OneView free?

Yes. The free plan covers one workspace with 3 accounts of your choice among YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Twitch, your combined stats for the last hour, 24 hours and 7 days, a page per account (Twitch subs, Bits and clips included), live status and viewers, the revenue chart (YouTube, plus Twitch and TikTok estimates), weekly and monthly reports and milestone notifications. OneView Pro costs $3.99 a month or $24.99 a year (or $49.99 once for life), and eligible new subscribers get the first 7 days free.

Can I publish one video to YouTube, TikTok and Instagram at once?

Yes, with Pro. Pick a video, write the title, description and hashtags once, tick your accounts, and set each platform's options: visibility, made for kids, schedule and playlist on YouTube, who can watch or a draft in your TikTok inbox, Reel or Story on Instagram, plus a thumbnail. YouTube itself decides whether a video is a Short: vertical or square videos of 3 minutes or less become Shorts.

Can I reply to comments from OneView?

Yes, for YouTube and Instagram: each platform has its own tab with All, Unanswered and Possible bots, loaded from the newest comment to the oldest as you scroll. You can reply, save quick replies and remove spam. TikTok does not give apps access to comments, and Twitch clips have none; with Pro, your YouTube and Twitch live chat can be read and answered from the account page while you're live.

Does OneView work with Twitch?

Yes. Connect your channel on Twitch's own activation page: OneView shows your followers, subs, Bits, your clips and their views and your estimated revenue, with a red LIVE badge, your viewers and how long you've been live. With Pro: subs by tier and gifted subs, top cheerers, the Hype Train and your Twitch goals for any period, the Hype Train and your sub goal live, and your live chat right in the app. Twitch has no view total, so OneView counts your clips' views. Subs and Bits need Affiliate or Partner status.

Can I see my Twitch and TikTok revenue?

Yes, as estimates, always marked as such: Twitch's is your subs × their price × your share (50/50, 60/40, 70/30 or your own, set in Profile) plus 1 cent per Bit; TikTok's counts the views of your videos of a minute or more since you're monetized (10K followers), at your own rate if you type it, otherwise as a range. Both are added to your real YouTube revenue in the totals, with an "of which estimated" line.

Can I repost my Twitch clips?

Yes, with Pro. On your Twitch page, pick a clip (its button, a long press, or its page): it opens in New post as your video, in portrait. Give it a title and a description, tick your YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts, and it's posted as a Short, TikTok or Reel. Twitch's API doesn't let apps delete a clip, so that stays on Twitch.

Does OneView see my passwords or sell my data?

No. You sign in on Google, TikTok, Meta or Twitch's own pages, and OneView never sees your password. Your tokens, stats and goals stay on your phone, nothing is sold or shared, and videos you publish go straight from your phone to the platform.

Can I manage several channels or clients?

Yes. Group accounts into workspaces, for example your own channels and each client, and switch from the Home screen. Pro removes the limits on accounts and workspaces.

Can I see how much I earn on YouTube?

Yes. Connect your YouTube channel and tap the $ button above the chart: your estimated revenue for the period, its chart, and on your channel's page the RPM and CPM. Each video's page shows its own revenue. Twitch and TikTok don't share revenue with apps: OneView estimates it (see below) and adds it to your total, marked as estimated. Instagram has none.

Why can I see some stats on one platform and not another?

OneView brings four different platforms into one app, and each one shares different data with apps. Only YouTube gives its real revenue, RPM and CPM (Twitch and TikTok revenue are OneView's estimates, always marked as such); TikTok and Twitch don't let apps manage comments; Instagram doesn't give follower history; Twitch has no view total, so OneView counts your clips' views. When a platform doesn't share something, OneView never passes a guess off as a real figure.

Are reports free?

Yes. The weekly and the monthly report are free for everyone: a story-ready image of your views, followers and best post, to share with your community, a brand or a client.

How do I cancel OneView Pro?

Pro is a subscription bought in the app through the App Store or Google Play. Cancel at any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings, at least 24 hours before the renewal.

Is OneView affiliated with YouTube, TikTok or Instagram?

No. OneView is an independent app that uses the official YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Twitch APIs.
