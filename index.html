Questions, answered.

Everything people ask before installing OneView.

What is OneView?

OneView is a mobile app for creators that brings the stats of your YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts into one place: views, likes, comments and followers, one chart for every platform, goals, the best time to post, your comments, and one button to publish a video to all your accounts at once.

Which platforms and phones does OneView support?

OneView works with YouTube, TikTok and Instagram (professional accounts). The app runs on iPhone and Android, with Home Screen widgets on both.

Is OneView free?

Yes. The free plan covers one workspace with one YouTube, one TikTok and one Instagram account, your combined stats for the last hour, 24 hours and 7 days, a page per account, weekly reports and milestone notifications. OneView Pro costs €4.99 a month or €39.99 a year, and eligible new subscribers get the first 7 days free.

Can I publish one video to YouTube, TikTok and Instagram at once?

Yes, with Pro. Pick a video, write the title, description and hashtags once, tick your accounts, and set each platform's options: visibility, made for kids, schedule and playlist on YouTube, who can watch or a draft in your TikTok inbox, Reel or Story on Instagram, plus a thumbnail. YouTube itself decides whether a video is a Short: vertical or square videos of 3 minutes or less become Shorts.

Can I reply to comments from OneView?

Yes, for YouTube and Instagram: each platform has its own tab with All, Unanswered and Possible bots, loaded from the newest comment to the oldest as you scroll. You can reply, save quick replies and remove spam. TikTok does not give apps access to comments.

Does OneView see my passwords or sell my data?

No. You sign in on Google, TikTok or Meta's own pages, and OneView never sees your password. Your tokens, stats and goals stay on your phone, nothing is sold or shared, and videos you publish go straight from your phone to the platform.

Can I manage several channels or clients?

Yes. Group accounts into workspaces, for example your own channels and each client, and switch from the Home screen. Pro removes the limits on accounts and workspaces.

How do I cancel OneView Pro?

Pro is a subscription bought in the app through the App Store or Google Play. Cancel at any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings, at least 24 hours before the renewal.

Is OneView affiliated with YouTube, TikTok or Instagram?

No. OneView is an independent app that uses the official YouTube, TikTok and Instagram APIs.
