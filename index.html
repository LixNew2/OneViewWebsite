Questions, answered.

Everything people ask before installing OneView.

What is OneView?

OneView is a mobile app for creators that brings the stats of your YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Twitch accounts into one place: views, followers, revenue and lives, one chart for every platform, goals, the best time to post, your comments, and one button to publish a video (or your Twitch clips) to all your accounts at once.

Which platforms and phones does OneView support?

OneView works with YouTube, TikTok, Instagram (professional accounts) and Twitch. The app runs on iPhone and Android, with Home Screen widgets on both.

Is OneView free?

Yes, and it does a lot. The free plan covers one workspace with 3 accounts of your choice among YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Twitch, your combined stats for the last hour, 24 hours and 7 days, a page per account (Twitch subs, Bits and clips included), live status and viewers, the revenue chart (YouTube, plus Twitch and TikTok estimates), 3 publications a week to all your accounts at once, 20 comment replies a week, 1 goal, one stats widget, your own figures in every Analyses card (best slot to post, your best post, words that work, period comparisons, followers per 1,000 views, the same video on every platform, posting pace, videos that last, moments to clip), weekly and monthly reports, the media kit and milestone notifications. OneView Pro removes every limit and shows every card in full, for $3.99 a month or $24.99 a year (or $49.99 once for life), and eligible new subscribers get the first 7 days free.

Can I publish one video to YouTube, TikTok and Instagram at once?

Yes, 3 times a week on the free plan, without limit with Pro. Pick a video, write the title, description and hashtags once, tick your accounts, and set each platform's options: visibility, made for kids and playlist on YouTube (scheduling with Pro), who can watch or a draft in your TikTok inbox, Reel or Story on Instagram, plus a thumbnail. One publication counts once, however many accounts it goes to. YouTube itself decides whether a video is a Short: vertical or square videos of 3 minutes or less become Shorts.

Can I reply to comments from OneView?

Yes, for YouTube and Instagram: each platform has its own tab with All, Unread, Unanswered and Possible bots, loaded from the newest comment to the oldest as you scroll. The free plan replies to 20 comments a week; Pro replies without limit, with quick replies and the bot filter. TikTok does not give apps access to comments, and Twitch clips have none; with Pro, your YouTube and Twitch live chat can be read and answered from the account page while you're live.

What can OneView show that YouTube, TikTok or Instagram can't?

Each platform only sees itself; OneView sees all of them. It shows the same video side by side on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram and which platform your videos do best on (sure when OneView published it, probable otherwise, and you confirm), the followers you win for every 1,000 views on each platform, whether posting more often lowers the views of each post, your older posts that still gain views (to publish again in one tap), the moments of your long YouTube videos viewers watch again (from the retention curve, with their timecodes, to cut into Shorts) and, with Pro, a notification as soon as a new post takes off against your usual curve. Free: each card shows its first result with your own figures. Pro: every card in full.

What is the media kit?

Your audience on every platform on one image, to send to a brand by mail or DM: total audience, views over 30 days, engagement, posts per week, followers, average views and engagement per platform, and your best posts. It is made on your phone and nothing is put online: there is no public page and nobody can look your stats up. Free for everyone with a "Stats from OneView" line; with Pro, add your contact and rates and take the line away.

Does OneView work with Twitch?

Yes. Connect your channel on Twitch's own activation page: OneView shows your followers, subs, Bits, your clips and their views and your estimated revenue, with a red LIVE badge, your viewers and how long you've been live. With Pro: subs by tier and gifted subs, top cheerers, the Hype Train and your Twitch goals for any period, the Hype Train and your sub goal live, and your live chat right in the app. Twitch has no view total, so OneView counts your clips' views. Subs and Bits need Affiliate or Partner status.

Can I see my Twitch and TikTok revenue?

Yes, as estimates, always marked as such: Twitch's is your subs × their price × your share (50/50, 60/40, 70/30 or your own, set in Profile) plus 1 cent per Bit; TikTok's counts the views of your videos of a minute or more since you're monetized (10K followers), at your own rate if you type it, otherwise as a range. Both are added to your real YouTube revenue in the totals, with an "of which estimated" line.

Can I repost my Twitch clips?

Yes, with Pro. On your Twitch page, pick a clip (its button, a long press, or its page): it opens in New post as your video, in portrait. Give it a title and a description, tick your YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts, and it's posted as a Short, TikTok or Reel. Twitch's API doesn't let apps delete a clip, so that stays on Twitch.

Does OneView see my passwords or sell my data?

No. You sign in on Google, TikTok, Meta or Twitch's own pages, and OneView never sees your password. Your tokens, stats and goals stay on your phone, nothing is sold or shared, and videos you publish go straight from your phone to the platform.

Can I manage several channels or clients?

Yes. Group accounts into workspaces, for example your own channels and each client, and switch from the Home screen. Pro removes the limits on accounts and workspaces.

Can I see how much I earn on YouTube?

Yes. Connect your YouTube channel and tap the $ button above the chart: your estimated revenue for the period, its chart, and on your channel's page the RPM and CPM. Each video's page shows its own revenue. Twitch and TikTok don't share revenue with apps: OneView estimates it (see below) and adds it to your total, marked as estimated. Instagram has none.

Why can I see some stats on one platform and not another?

OneView brings four different platforms into one app, and each one shares different data with apps. Only YouTube gives its real revenue, RPM and CPM (Twitch and TikTok revenue are OneView's estimates, always marked as such); TikTok and Twitch don't let apps manage comments; Instagram doesn't give follower history; Twitch has no view total, so OneView counts your clips' views. When a platform doesn't share something, OneView never passes a guess off as a real figure.

Are reports free?

Yes. The weekly and the monthly report are free for everyone: a story-ready image of your views, followers and best post, to share with your community, a brand or a client.

How do I cancel OneView Pro?

Pro is a subscription bought in the app through the App Store or Google Play. Cancel at any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings, at least 24 hours before the renewal.

Is OneView affiliated with YouTube, TikTok or Instagram?

No. OneView is an independent app that uses the official YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Twitch APIs.

Can I see the same video's views on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram side by side?

Yes. The "Same video, everywhere" card puts the views of one video on every platform next to each other and shows the platform where it did best. Videos published with OneView are linked for sure; others posted within 3 days with a similar title are matched as probable, and you confirm or refuse each match. Over several videos, OneView tells you which platform gets the same video the most views.

What are the moments to clip?

For long YouTube videos (3 minutes or more), OneView reads YouTube's audience retention curve and finds the parts viewers rewind to watch again. Those are the best moments to cut into a Short, a Reel or a TikTok. OneView gives their timecodes and opens the video right at that moment; it does not download or cut the video, you cut it in your own editor. It is YouTube only, since TikToks, Reels and clips are already short.

What does "Views into followers" show?

How many followers you win for every 1,000 views on each platform over the period, so you can see where your views turn into a community. Pick a platform or one account at the top of Analyses and the card shows each account instead.

Does posting more often lower my views?

The posting pace card answers that from your own history: it groups your weeks by how many posts you made (1 to 2, 3 to 4, 5 to 7, 8 or more) and shows how each post did against your usual in those weeks. It needs about six weeks of posts.

What are videos that last?

Your posts of 30 days or more that still gain views every week. They are good candidates to publish again as a Short, a Reel or a TikTok, and OneView opens a new post with their title in one tap.

Which Home Screen widgets does OneView have?

Three, on iPhone and Android: a stats widget (views, followers, revenue, Twitch subs or Bits for a workspace, a platform or one account, over any period), a goal widget, and a Latest video widget that shows your newest video with its views curve, likes, comments, engagement, followers won and how it compares with your usual. The Latest video widget has an automatic mode that shows the most viewed of your latest videos across all the accounts of a workspace.

Can I manage several YouTube channels or Instagram accounts?

Yes. Connect several accounts of the same platform and group them into workspaces. Wherever OneView shows one account's figures, it names the account when a workspace has more than one of that platform, and Analyses lets you pick all the accounts of a platform or just one.

How often are my stats updated?

Every few minutes while OneView is open, and in the background as often as your phone allows (about every 15 minutes or more). YouTube's own analytics arrive about two days late; OneView fills the gap with the live view count.

Can I schedule posts?

Yes, for YouTube: pick a date and time when you publish and the video stays private until then. TikTok and Instagram are published right away.

Will OneView tell me when a video takes off?

Yes. You get a notification when a post reaches 3 times your usual views, when you pass a follower milestone, when you reach a goal and, with Pro, as soon as a new post beats your usual curve hour by hour.

Can I export my stats?

Yes, with Pro: a CSV file of your accounts, posts and day-by-day history, to open in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets. The export uses the figures already on your phone and makes no extra request.

Which languages is OneView in?

English, French and Spanish. The app follows your phone's language, and your quick replies to comments follow it too.

Can I reply to TikTok comments?

Not yet: TikTok does not let apps read or answer comments on regular creator accounts. YouTube and Instagram comments are fully supported.

Does OneView work with a personal Instagram account?

Instagram only shares stats with apps for professional accounts. Switch to a free Creator or Business account in Instagram's settings, then connect it to OneView.

What happens if I stop OneView Pro?

Your accounts, goals and settings stay on your phone. The Pro features lock again (only your first goal stays on, longer periods and detailed analytics close) and come back as soon as you subscribe again.

Can I try OneView Pro for free?

Yes. Eligible new subscribers get the first 7 days free, and you can cancel before the end of the trial in your App Store or Google Play settings without being charged.

Is there a limit on the free plan?

The free plan covers 3 accounts in one workspace, 3 publications and 20 comment replies a week, one goal, one stats widget, and the first result of every Analyses card with your own figures. Pro removes every limit.
